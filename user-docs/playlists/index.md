# Playlists & Favorites

Favorites and playlists let you curate across games. Both belong to your
account — sign in with Google and they follow you to any device that signs
in to the same instance.

## Favorites

Every track row has a heart. Click it and the track lands in your favorites;
click again to remove it. Your favorites are a regular playlist view — open
them from the sidebar and play them like anything else.

::: tip Nothing is lost when you sign out
Favorites are stored server-side against your account. Sign back in on any
device and they are exactly where you left them.
:::

## Playlists

- Open the **add-to-playlist** button on any track row: a popup lists your
  playlists and toggles the track's membership in each.
- Create playlists with the **create** button — Trackify creates the
  playlist and opens it, ready to fill and rename.
- **Rename** a playlist you own by clicking its title in the hero panel —
  edit in place, `Enter` commits, `Escape` cancels.

## The now-playing view

The `now-playing` view mirrors exactly what the player bar is currently
playing — useful when you want to look at the queue, or send someone a link
to "what's on right now".

## Who can see what

| Content            | Visible to                        |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Games & tracks     | everyone (no account needed)      |
| Your favorites     | you, after signing in             |
| Your playlists     | you, after signing in             |

Catalog administration (adding games, editing metadata, uploads) is
superuser-only — see [Administration](/admin/).
