# Browsing Collections

The collection browser is the front page of Trackify: every game in the
catalog appears as a card with its cover art. Click any game to open its
album view.

## The layout

```
+------------------------------------------------------------+
|  topbar        [search]                     [account]      |
+----------+-------------------------------------------------+
| sidebar  |                                                 |
|          |   game grid (cover art cards)                   |
| Collect- |                                                 |
| ions     |   +------+  +------+  +------+                  |
| ...      |   |cover |  |cover |  |cover |                  |
|          |   +------+  +------+  +------+                  |
+----------+-------------------------------------------------+
|  player bar (always visible at the bottom)                 |
+------------------------------------------------------------+
```

- **Sidebar** — main navigation between pages; the active entry is
  highlighted as you move around.
- **Topbar** — search and your account state.
- **Player bar** — pinned to the bottom, it keeps playing while you browse.
  See [The Player & Formats](/player/).

## Search

The search field in the topbar searches **tracks** across the whole library.
Results appear in a popover as playable rows (cover art, title, game): click
a row to start playing immediately, or follow the game link to open that
game's album view.

## Collections: games, platforms, artists, playlists

Besides the game grid, the collections pages let you browse the library from
other angles:

- **Games** — the cover-art wall.
- **Platforms** — one entry per console/platform; open one to see its games.
- **Artists** — one entry per composer/artist; open one to see every track by
  that artist across your whole library.
- **Playlists** — your own playlists (plus your favorites) when you are
  signed in.

## The album view

Clicking a game opens its **album view**: a hero panel with cover art, title
and metadata, followed by the full track list. Click a track to play it; the
player bar takes over from there.

From the album view you can also:

- **Heart a track** to add it to your favorites (sign-in required) — see
  [Playlists & Favorites](/playlists/).
- **Add a track to a playlist** via the row's add-to-playlist button.

## Where URLs come from

Every view is addressable, so you can bookmark or share deep links: game
albums, platform and artist pages, your favorites, individual playlists, and
a now-playing view that mirrors the player's current queue.
